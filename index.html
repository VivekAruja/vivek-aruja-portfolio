13-Bus Contingency Analysis

Independent Project, PowerWorld + Tableau

Modeled a simplified 13-bus, Ontario-inspired transmission network in PowerWorld Simulator and ran N-1 contingency analysis, solving the base case with Newton-Raphson power flow, then opening one transmission element at a time across nine outage cases spanning generation, transformer, and transfer paths.

  • 9 contingency cases tested
  • 5 of 9 classified severe
  • Worst case, Bruce Generation → GTA West: 0.645 pu voltage, 305.5% branch loading

Recorded voltage violations, thermal overloads, and severity classifications per case, built corrective action recommendations, and summarized results in a Tableau dashboard and technical report.
